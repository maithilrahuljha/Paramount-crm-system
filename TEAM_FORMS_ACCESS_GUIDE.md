## ✅ Team Rules

1. **Every enquiry gets a form entry** — no lead lives only in your notebook.
2. **Lead IDs** (`PMN-YYYY-XXXX`) are auto-generated — copy them from the
   Lead_Register sheet when logging follow-ups.
3. **Priority levels:**
   - **P1** — Admission within 3 days → call TODAY
   - **P2** — Interested → call within 48 hours
   - **P3** — Needs counselling → schedule a session
   - **P4** — Future batch → monthly check-in
   - **P5** — Cold lead → quarterly nurture
4. **Daily report is mandatory** — the 8:30 AM briefing is built from it.
5. Never edit the sheets directly — always go through the forms.
